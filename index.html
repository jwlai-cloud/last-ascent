The Last Ascent

You cannot stop climbing and the storm never stops rising. Swipe to change lane, tap to jump a gap. Collect energy — it is your score and your only fuel, so every point spent surviving comes off the run. Hazards make you slip. And energy is worth more the closer the storm is — ×2 within three floors, ×4 within one and a half. Climb too fast and you outrun your own pay. Every four floors the lanes split into safe, danger and unknown. Reach floor 20 and keep what you did not spend.
