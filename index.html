SURVIVAL & RESOURCE MANAGEMENT

The Last Ascent

Someone is stranded at the top of this tower, and the storm is climbing faster than they can wait. Nothing carries you up. Press SPACE (or tap) and you leap one level; stand still and the tower falls away beneath you. Steer with A / D or a swipe — you can steer in mid-air, so jump on the beat and pick where you land. One perk, AIR SAVE, buys a second press while you are falling — that is why a double tap sometimes carries you over a hole; the cone on his head glows while it is still available and greys out the moment you spend it. Every perk you take is something he wears — cone on the head, halo, boots, gloves, shoulder pips — and it pops as you take it so you can see which is which. Watch the climber, not this text: a red ring at his feet means danger in your lane right now, and a violet spinning ring means the tower is about to turn. Land on spikes and it costs a life and your footing; fall below the storm line and it costs a life too. Energy is your score and your fuel, and it pays ×2 within three levels of the storm, ×4 within one and a half — so climbing too fast outruns your own pay. Gold is always worth taking. Every few levels the lanes split into safe, danger and unknown, each holding a different upgrade — and the tower sometimes turns, swapping which side each lane is on, with a second and a half of warning. Three lives — and one spend: S (or the button) buys a SHIELD that eats the next hit, straight out of your score. The tower gets nastier the higher you go. Every thirtieth level banks your score and patches one life back. Reach 300, get to them, and keep the lot.
